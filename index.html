About Us

MEG AVERKS TECHNOLOGIES

Based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, MeGaVenTs designs, manufactures, installs and services air pollution control and bulk material handling equipment for agro-processing and heavy industries.

From a single cyclone to a complete turnkey dust extraction plant, every system is engineered for reliability, efficiency and compliance with pollution control norms — and backed by responsive after-sales service.

  • In-house design & fabrication facility
  • Complete installation & commissioning by our own team
  • Dependable after-sales support — AMC, spares & breakdown service
  • Custom-built to your plant layout and capacity
  • Systems compliant with CPCB / KSPCB norms
