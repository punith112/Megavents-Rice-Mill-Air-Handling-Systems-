About MeGaVenTs

MEG AVERKS TECHNOLOGIES (MeGaVenTs) is a Bengaluru-based manufacturer of air pollution control and bulk material handling equipment, serving rice mills, dal mills, flour mills, cement plants and process industries across India.

From a single cyclone separator to a complete turnkey dust extraction plant, every system is engineered in-house for reliability, efficiency and compliance with CPCB / KSPCB pollution control norms. With our own fabrication facility at Magadi Road and office at Rajajinagar Industrial Estate, we control quality at every step — and stand behind every installation with dependable after-sales service.

What We Manufacture

Our Products — Dust Collection

Pulse Jet Bag Filters

Online pulse-jet dust collectors with filter bags and cages for fine particulate control, helping plants meet KSPCB / CPCB emission norms.

Cyclone Separators

High-efficiency cyclones for coarse dust and husk separation in rice mills, dal mills, flour mills and wood-working units. Low pressure drop design.

Dust Extraction Systems

Complete turnkey dust extraction plants with ducting, suction hoods and spark arrestors — from design to commissioning.

Rotary Airlock Valves

Heavy-duty rotary feeders for controlled, air-tight discharge of powders and granules from cyclones, hoppers and bag filters. MS / SS, custom sizes.

What We Manufacture

Our Products — Conveying & Air Handling

Pneumatic Conveying Systems

Lean and dense phase conveying for grains, flour, cement, ash and other bulk materials — dust-free transfer with minimal manpower.

Centrifugal Fans & Blowers

ID / FD fans and high-pressure blowers for dust extraction, husk conveying and process air. Dynamically balanced impellers.

Axial Flow Fans

High-volume axial fans for ventilation and fume extraction in factories, warehouses and processing plants.

Slide Gate Valves

Manual and pneumatic slide gates for positive shut-off in hoppers, silos and conveying lines. Built for abrasive materials.
